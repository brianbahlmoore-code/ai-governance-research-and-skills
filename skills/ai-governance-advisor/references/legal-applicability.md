# Global legal applicability and currency

Research checked: 2026-10-23. This is a source-led issue-spotting workflow. It is not a full jurisdictional legal opinion. Read [sources.md](sources.md) and refresh the applicable rules before making a current compliance determination.

## Applicability before requirements

Determine relevant entities, countries, markets, affected people, organizational roles, intended purpose, sector, data types, deployment date and material changes. A single enterprise can hold different roles for different activities. The label used by a vendor contract may not determine the statutory role.

Build a register with: instrument and version; jurisdiction; authoritative provision; actor; triggering facts; obligation; effective/application dates; exceptions; evidence needed; accountable owner; confidence; legal-review status. Distinguish source text from interpretation. Do not equate an internal high-risk tier with the EU AI Act's high-risk category.

## EU

Use the AI Act and relevant amendments for role, prohibited-practice, high-risk, transparency and general-purpose-model analysis. Check exceptions, transitional provisions and product-specific laws. Do not classify every use of a general-purpose model as high-risk or assume a provider's compliance discharges deployer obligations.

As of the research date, the Commission's official materials identify Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 as the AI Omnibus amendment and report revised high-risk application dates: 2 December 2027 for Annex III and 2 August 2028 for relevant Annex I product systems. Other obligations have separate dates; this is not a blanket delay. The official explorer says its consolidated baseline is 27 July 2026. An attempted direct HTML retrieval from EUR-Lex was blocked; the Official Journal PDF was available through search. Verify exact provisions in the authoritative text for a live case. [L1-L3]

Privacy analysis should cover lawful processing, purposes, minimization, sensitive data, individual rights, processors, international transfers, security and impact assessments where applicable. For automated decisions, determine the actual legal trigger; 'a human is involved somewhere' does not resolve the analysis. Separate AI-system impact work from a statutory DPIA or other required assessment. The GDPR primary-text link is provided for verification; full text was not retrieved in this research. [L4]

## United States

Map federal and state duties by activity and geography rather than implying one omnibus private-sector AI law. Employment, credit, housing, consumer protection, privacy, biometrics, intellectual property and regulated products can matter independently of AI-specific legislation. Retrieve current regulator guidance and applicable law before asserting a state-law deadline, exception or remedy.

EO 14110 was rescinded on 20 January 2025. Do not carry it forward as an active legal obligation from older study materials. Historical references to it in a NIST publication explain that publication's origin and do not automatically invalidate the publication. [L5]

For financial institutions, SR 26-2 of 17 April 2026 supersedes SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. Confirm model scope, applicability and the institution's regulator; do not assume every GenAI system fits the supervisory definition without examining the guidance. The 2023 third-party guidance is a useful sector source, while a 2026 replacement proposal must be tracked separately until finalized. [F1-F3]

## South Korea

Official MSIT material confirms the AI Basic Act and Enforcement Decree took effect on 22 January 2026. Transparency guidance addresses prior notice for certain high-impact or generative AI services and labeling of generated content. July 2026 amendments are also signaled by MSIT; verify the latest consolidated law, decree and implementation guidance before detailed advice. Do not assume an initial English translation incorporates every amendment. [K1-K3]

## Other markets and sectors

Use OECD and UNESCO as governance lenses, not substitutes for national law. For Singapore, distinguish voluntary governance guidance from binding laws. For any additional country, inspect official sources for privacy, automated decisions, employment, consumer, sector and AI-specific obligations. The user's location alone does not establish which law governs their enterprise or vendors.

## Currency and conflicts

Recheck sources at assessment start and before final legal conclusions. Record publication, amendment, application and retrieval dates separately. If an official overview and legal text conflict, investigate the controlling text, scope and transition rather than silently choosing a date. If access is unavailable, say what is unverified and continue with bounded analysis. For exam preparation, maintain a separate exam-baseline field; never assume newly enacted law is already tested unless IAPP confirms the tested scope.
