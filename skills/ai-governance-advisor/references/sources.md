# Research source register

Checked 2026-10-23. This register distinguishes sources inspected from links retained for later verification. Public summaries cannot establish every requirement in a licensed standard. Search snippets were used for certain availability/status checks; they are not a substitute for the full controlling text in a formal assessment.

## Certification

- **A1** [IAPP AIGP certification](https://www.iapp.org/certify/aigp) — official credential scope and preparation-resource index; inspected.
- **A2** [AIGP BoK v2.1, effective 2 February 2026](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltd4dd5b2d705252bc/blt579bac3f0b35f278/69494b068965be6043ba2815/AIGP_BOK_2.1.0_FINAL.pdf) — official public blueprint; inspected, with the user's local v2.1 copy used for full coverage mapping.
- **A3** [IAPP certification FAQs](https://www.iapp.org/certify/faqs) — format, scoring and handbook link; inspected. The text has potentially confusing descriptions of scaling; report 300 on the 100-500 reporting scale without interpreting it as a percentage.
- **A4** [IAPP exam listing](https://store.iapp.org/aigp-exam/) — inspected listing: USD 649 member / 799 nonmember, 100 questions, 2.75 hours plus reference to a 15-minute break, one-year purchase window, two-year maintenance term and 20 continuing-education credits. Confirm checkout and candidate instructions before purchase/scheduling.
- **A5** [IAPP free study guide](https://pages.iapp.org/rs/138-EZM-042/images/AIGP%20FSG.pdf?version=4) — public guide; inspected relevant search text and local supplied resources. Describes three hours; the current FAQ lists 2.75 hours. Treat 165 minutes as practice working time, and verify break/session rules with the candidate instructions.

## Maintenance protocol

Before revising a source-derived rule, record old source/version, new source/version, change, affected guidance and whether templates or training questions need revision. Preserve historical dates when answering historical questions. Prefer a current official source over a community skill's 'last verified' label. No automatic background update or external upload is configured.
