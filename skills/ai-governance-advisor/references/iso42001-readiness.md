# ISO/IEC 42001 readiness and assurance

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 addresses an organizational AI management system. ISO 23894 supports AI risk management; ISO 42005 supports system impact assessment; ISO 22989 supports terminology. Public ISO scope descriptions were checked on 2026-10-23. Full licensed normative text was not available for this research. [S1-S4 in sources.md]

The following is an original preparation workflow. Treat exact clause/control references and required wording as needing verification against the organization's applicable licensed edition, including amendments. Do not label this file a complete control catalog or substitute for the standard.

## Define what is being assessed

State legal entities, locations, business processes, AI systems and activities inside the AIMS boundary. Record provider/user roles and outsourced responsibilities. Explain exclusions and dependencies. A certificate limited to one business unit cannot be represented as coverage of every vendor product.

Choose the engagement: preliminary gap assessment, implementation planning, internal audit or certification readiness. Define criteria, evidence period, sampling and assessor independence. A preparatory gap assessment does not confer certification.

## Evidence workstreams

| Workstream | Practical preparation evidence | Common false positive |
|---|---|---|
| Organizational context and scope | Stakeholder needs, boundaries, obligations and dependencies | A broad scope statement with untracked outsourced activities |
| Leadership | Approved policy, accountability, resources and decision records | A committee name without authority or attendance |
| Planning | Risk method, assessments, treatment priorities and objectives | An uncalibrated risk score with no rationale |
| Support | Competence, training, communications and document control | Attendance counts without competence evidence |
| Operation | Assessments, supplier reviews, validation, release and change records | Procedures that have never been used |
| Evaluation | Monitoring, internal assurance and management review | A dashboard that never produces an action |
| Improvement | Findings, corrective actions and effectiveness checks | Closing a finding after updating a document only |

These topics broadly track the management-system architecture; exact conformity criteria must come from the authoritative standard. Retain the distinction between management-system requirements and selected controls.

## Control applicability

Build a Statement of Applicability using the verified reference control set and any additional controls the organization needs. For each control record identifier, applicable scope, rationale for inclusion or exclusion, implementation state, responsible owner and evidence. Exclusion needs a reason tied to risk and scope; 'we buy the model' does not remove all AI responsibilities.

Distinguish: requirement applies; control selected; control designed; control implemented; operation tested; effectiveness established. A selected control can be inadequately implemented. A documented exclusion can be unjustified. A generic checklist can miss organization-specific controls.

## Risk assessment versus impact assessment

The risk assessment supports management decisions about uncertain outcomes and treatment. The impact assessment examines consequences for people, groups and society, including those who never use the product. They may share facts and evidence but should not collapse into an exclusively financial risk register. Neither automatically fulfills a jurisdiction-specific statutory assessment.

For a purchased AI system, investigate what the provider assessed, what remains relevant in the customer's context, what is unknown and what the deployer must evaluate independently. Broad vendor claims do not replace local impact analysis.

## Audit method

Trace a sample from intake through classification, assessment, treatment, testing, decision, production monitoring and a material change. Interview owners and inspect actual records. Include a consequential use case, a third-party service and an incident or exception when available. Select sample size based on scope, diversity and risk; do not promise full assurance from one sample.

For each finding use: verified criterion; objective evidence; observed gap; significance; responsible owner; correction; cause analysis; corrective action; effectiveness test. Distinguish a substantiated nonconformity from missing evidence and from a suggestion. Use the organization's audit severity definitions instead of inventing a universal major/minor rule.

Management review should result in decisions on performance, risk, objectives, resources and improvement. Keep records of resulting actions and whether they were effective. Stage 1 and Stage 2 readiness can be organized around documented design and demonstrated operation, but the certification body's actual scope and procedures govern its assessment.

## Readiness output

State what was examined and what was not. Present substantiated gaps, unverified criteria and a sequenced remediation plan. Identify where a licensed-standard review is still required. Never convert a percentage of checked boxes into a claim that certification will be achieved or that the organization meets all AI laws.
