# Vendor risk sources and limits

Baseline 2026-10-23. Detailed operating procedures and questionnaire wording in this skill are original.

- [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf): supplier/component risk is part of the broader risk framework; inspect relevant GOVERN, MAP and MANAGE outcomes for exact mappings.
- [NIST GenAI Profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf): generative AI and value-chain risks.
- [ISO 42001 public scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001): management-system context. Full licensed criteria were not reviewed; no exact normative control mapping is asserted.
- [OWASP LLM Top 10](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/): 2025 security baseline checked.
- [Federal Reserve 2023 third-party guidance](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/interagency-guidance-on-third-party-relationships.htm): banking lifecycle reference, not a universal enterprise law.
- [2026 TPRM proposal](https://occ.treas.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-ia-2026-77.html): a proposal at research time; verify later status.
- [SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm): current checked model-risk guidance superseding SR 11-7; evaluate actual applicability.
- [GDPR primary text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng): verification entry point; direct retrieval was blocked. Do not treat template language as a current legal opinion.
- [EU AI Act official explorer](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act-explorer): role-specific obligations and current consolidation entry point.
- [Community TPRM input](https://github.com/sushegaad/claude-skills-governance-risk-and-compliance/tree/aab13e1eb1dbf3b537b66f98d9b5eb52db0a7c25/plugins/tprm): previously reviewed inspiration for assurance/lifecycle structure; no files or code copied.

Assurance-report interpretation here is practitioner guidance; use the actual auditor report and applicable assurance criteria for a formal conclusion. The supplier's documents must match the evaluated entity, service, time period and configuration. Refresh legal terms and guidance at decision time.
