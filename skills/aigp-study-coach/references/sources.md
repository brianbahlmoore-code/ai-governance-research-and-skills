# Study sources and version rules

Checked 2026-10-23. The official blueprint remains the authority for exam scope; original lessons and practice questions in this pack are supplementary.

- [IAPP AIGP](https://www.iapp.org/certify/aigp): credential and preparation resources.
- [Official v2.1 blueprint](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltd4dd5b2d705252bc/blt579bac3f0b35f278/69494b068965be6043ba2815/AIGP_BOK_2.1.0_FINAL.pdf): four domains, 13 competencies, effective 2 February 2026. All 58 indicators mapped using the user's supplied local copy. Local objective suffixes in the CSV are this pack's identifiers.
- [Certification FAQs](https://www.iapp.org/certify/faqs): format, multi-select, reporting scale and candidate-handbook link.
- [Exam listing](https://store.iapp.org/aigp-exam/): pricing, purchase window and maintenance terms.
- [Free study guide](https://pages.iapp.org/rs/138-EZM-042/images/AIGP%20FSG.pdf?version=4): logistics wording differs from FAQ; confirm current candidate instructions.
- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and [Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): version and function/category/subcategory coverage.
- [NIST Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/): optional implementation guidance.
- [ISO 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001), [42005](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005), [22989](https://www.iso.org/standard/74296.html), [23894](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html): public scope descriptions only. Full standards are not reproduced or claimed to have been reviewed.
- [OECD AI Principles](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html): checked updated-2024 principles baseline.
- [Official EU AI Act explorer](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act-explorer): current legal-practice reference, not proof of immediate exam adoption of amendments.
- [Korea MSIT commencement](https://www.msit.go.kr/eng/bbs/view.do?bbsSeqNo=42&mId=4&nttSeqNo=1214&sCode=eng) and [transparency guidance](https://www.msit.go.kr/eng/bbs/view.do?bbsSeqNo=42&mId=4&nttSeqNo=1215&sCode=eng): core 2026 legal context; verify consolidated amendments for live advice.

The workspace contains older course materials. The supplied v2.1 blueprint, rather than the older v2.0.1 file or third-party course title, drove coverage. No proprietary slides, course book or official sample questions were copied into the skills.

When a learner asks about a historical rule, explain it as historical. When preparing for a future exam, verify the applicable blueprint. Avoid asserting that every legal development since publication is tested. Source discrepancies should be explained instead of hidden.
