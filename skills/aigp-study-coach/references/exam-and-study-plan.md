# AIGP certification and study plan

Research baseline: 2026-10-23. Verify the current [IAPP certification page](https://www.iapp.org/certify/aigp), [FAQ](https://www.iapp.org/certify/faqs), [exam listing](https://store.iapp.org/aigp-exam/) and candidate handbook before scheduling.

## Verified logistics snapshot

- The checked blueprint is v2.1, effective 2 February 2026.
- Current FAQ/listing: 100 questions and 2.75 hours; the listing also identifies a 15-minute break. The free guide describes three hours. Use 165 minutes of working time for practice and verify total session/break rules in the candidate instructions.
- Multiple-choice and scenario formats are used. The FAQ describes multi-select items with an exact number of choices and no partial credit.
- The reported passing score is 300 on a 100-500 scale; this is not a 60% raw pass mark.
- Listed exam price: USD 649 for members, USD 799 for nonmembers, subject to checkout, taxes and changes. The listing says the exam must be completed within one year of purchase.
- The listing describes a two-year certification term and 20 relevant continuing-education credits. Membership covers the maintenance fee; the listing says initial nonmember maintenance is included and later renewal is USD 250. Recheck current terms.

The official public materials differ in some logistical wording. Do not flatten those differences into a false certainty. No purchase, enrollment or scheduling is performed by this pack.

## Coverage architecture

| Domain | Blueprint question range | Suggested learning emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| I: Governance foundations | 16-20 | Concepts, accountable roles and lifecycle policies |
| II: Laws, standards and frameworks | 19-23 | Applicability and distinctions between instruments |
| III: Governing development | 21-25 | Design, data, evaluation, release and maintenance |
| IV: Governing deployment and use | 21-25 | Selection, supplier evaluation, operations and retirement |

Ranges are blueprint ranges, not fixed percentages of a 100-item paper. Do not normalize them into an asserted official exam allocation. The practice bank has two questions per competency to expose reasoning gaps; it is not an exam-weighted simulation.

## Proposed eight-week plan

Allow approximately 6-8 hours per week as an initial planning assumption, adjusted after the diagnostic. This is a coaching proposal, not an IAPP requirement or pass guarantee.

1. Foundations: distinguish AI types and risks; map accountability for a purchased AI assistant; assess current knowledge across domains.
2. Governance program: create a small inventory, role matrix, policy structure and risk appetite proposal; explain why principles need controls.
3. Laws and roles: practice applicability questions involving privacy, IP, discrimination, consumer protection and AI-specific rules. Use current primary sources.
4. Frameworks: compare AIGP, ISO 42001, ISO 23894, ISO 42005, NIST AI RMF, OECD and technical security guidance. Create one evidence-based crosswalk.
5. Development: design a representative evaluation, review data provenance, select fairness criteria and analyze a failed release decision.
6. Deployment and suppliers: assess a fictional AI vendor, identify contract gaps and distinguish inherited controls from customer responsibilities.
7. Operations: work through drift, model updates, complaints, incident response, downstream misuse and a shutdown/exit exercise.
8. Consolidation: use fresh mixed scenarios, review the error log, revisit unassessed objectives and rehearse timing. Verify current exam instructions.

## Retrieval and practice routine

For each study session: recall prior concepts without notes, study one weak topic, solve scenarios, explain wrong options and schedule a later revisit. Space revisits based on demonstrated retention. The learner should be able to identify the actor, lifecycle stage, decision, harm, evidence and strongest next action in an unfamiliar case.

Suggested internal mastery labels: unassessed; recognizes; explains; applies; transfers. Record the evidence behind the label. A suggested readiness signal is consistently sound reasoning on fresh mixed scenarios with no major domain gaps; any numerical target is a personal study heuristic, not an official cut score.

## Capstone

Use a global enterprise procuring an AI assistant that retrieves employee records and can submit HR requests. Produce: a use-case inventory row; role and jurisdiction questions; impact/risk assessment; vendor due diligence; data-use and change-control requirements; release evidence; monitoring; incident response; exit. Explain which artifacts support multiple frameworks and which gaps remain. The goal is to defend decisions, not merely name standards.
