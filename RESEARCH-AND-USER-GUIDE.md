## Main research conclusion

An effective enterprise program uses several complementary layers rather than choosing one framework to do every job:

1. **AIGP supplies the learning structure.** It develops individual knowledge of governance, law/frameworks, development and deployment. It does not certify an organization's products. [IAPP](https://www.iapp.org/certify/aigp)
2. **ISO 42001 supplies the management-system scope.** It concerns how an organization establishes and operates AI governance. Its public scope does not establish every normative requirement or certify every system as safe. [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001)
3. **NIST AI RMF supplies a flexible risk-management structure.** GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE connect accountability, context, evaluation and treatment. The functions are iterative and the Playbook is optional guidance. [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [Playbook FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/nist-ai-rmf-playbook-faqs)
4. **Supporting standards and technical guidance address specific needs.** ISO 23894 addresses AI risk guidance, ISO 42005 system impacts and ISO 22989 terminology. GenAI and agent deployments need additional architecture-specific assurance. [23894](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html), [42005](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005), [22989](https://www.iso.org/standard/74296.html)
5. **Applicable law and contracts establish binding duties.** These depend on role, location, purpose, data, sector and dates. Framework alignment is not proof of legal compliance.

Your practical record should connect use case, version, affected people, obligation, risk, control, test, evidence, residual-risk decision and monitoring. The pack implements that connection through shared concepts and templates, without asserting automatic equivalence between frameworks.

## AIGP coverage

The checked official baseline is BoK v2.1, effective 2 February 2026. The workspace's supplied v2.1 document was used to map all 58 indicators into original study objectives across 13 competency groups and four domains. The CSV uses local objective identifiers; the official competency labels remain recognizable.

This provides full syllabus-topic coverage, not the full proprietary IAPP course or a guaranteed substitute for all official study resources. The initial question bank samples every competency; it does not individually test all 58 objectives or simulate an exam's precise weighting. The coach can generate additional original exercises for each tracked gap.

The official FAQ/listing currently states 100 questions and 2.75 hours, while the study guide uses a three-hour description and the listing refers to a 15-minute break. The pack flags this difference and directs candidates to current instructions. It does not confuse the reporting score of 300 with a raw percentage. ## What changed from the community inputs

| Input | Useful idea retained | Improvement in this pack |
|---|---|---|
| AI risk management | Lifecycle framing and NIST orientation | Context-sensitive fairness; evidence states; uncertainty; system-level analysis; current-source checks |
| ISO 42001 | Scope, evidence and readiness | Explicit limits on unverified normative claims; separate design, implementation and effectiveness |
| TPRM | Assurance reports and vendor lifecycle | AI-specific data, behavior, terms, model changes, agents, fourth parties and exit |

No upstream files or executable code were copied. The reviewed source commits and provenance are recorded in the [source register](skills/ai-governance-advisor/references/sources.md).

Research identified material currency issues. EO 14110, listed in the community risk skill's regulatory section, was rescinded in January 2025. NIST reports its AI RMF revision is in progress. US banking model-risk guidance SR 26-2 superseded SR 11-7 in April 2026. EU official materials report amended high-risk application dates, which need role- and provision-specific verification in live assessments. The skills therefore refresh legal/version facts rather than treating this research snapshot as timeless. [EO notice](https://www.nist.gov/artificial-intelligence/executive-order-safe-secure-and-trustworthy-artificial-intelligence), [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm), [EU explorer](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act-explorer)

## How to use

Try these requests:

> Use $ai-governance-advisor to create a 90-day global AI governance program, including ownership, inventory, risk criteria and evidence requirements.

> Use $ai-third-party-risk to assess this AI vendor for our employee assistant. Separate confirmed evidence from claims and identify conditions for a pilot.

> Use $aigp-study-coach to diagnose my knowledge with five questions, then build a study plan and track gaps against the current syllabus.

For an ISO review, provide the authorized standard edition and actual organizational evidence when available. Without them, the skill produces a bounded readiness plan rather than an invented conformity result. For vendor work, provide the intended use and available terms/reports; the workflow can begin with explicit gaps when documents are missing.

## Boundaries of the research

Primary-source research focused on the named frameworks and high-value enterprise extensions. It is not an exhaustive legal survey of every country or a reproduction of paid standards. Some direct source fetches were blocked; the source register explicitly identifies where only public scope or indexed official material was available. Existing proprietary course slides/books were not copied or converted into the skills. The original AIGP practice bank is not endorsed by IAPP.

See [source register](skills/ai-governance-advisor/references/sources.md) for evidence and limitations, and [validation report](VALIDATION.md) for package checks.
